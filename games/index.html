    Games

    Here are some games I have worked on:

    Granny’s Gift

    An arcade simulation where Granny and Grandpa wrap Christmas gifts for their grandchildren using Unity and XBox Adaptive Controllers.

    A Gentlemen’s Dispute

    A friendslop party game where players fight each other as gentlemen until the last standing.

    A Decent Farewell

    A short VR experience where players play as an undertaker dressing up a corpse.

    Kung-Fu Penguin

    A party game using a special hardware where 4 players compete against each other to become the penguin king!

    Ginger Air

    A competitive game where two gingerbread man deliver as many gifts as possible to become the best helper for Santa!

    Mask.exe

    A horror puzzle game unveiling the secret of the program.
