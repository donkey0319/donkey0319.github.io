What I did

I was mainly working on the final 3D scene of the game. I worked on 3D modeling for all objects, texturing, lighting, and post-process filtering.

In addition to art, I also worked on the audio script and some program aspect of the game, including camera controlling and some tweening animations.

Overview

This is a game from the Global Game Jam in 2026, where the subject is MASK.

We designed a puzzle in which the players have to fill the area with blocks. If there are blocks that exceed the area, the players have to flip the blocks to MASK off the extra in order to proceed to the next level.
